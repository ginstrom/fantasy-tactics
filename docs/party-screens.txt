Encampment UI
=============

This document shows the encampment route in text form. Bracketed labels are
buttons. `TableView` is used whenever the player is choosing from a tabular
collection. It owns display, sorting, filtering, selection, and interaction
signals; `GameSession` remains the source of party and unit data.

TableView conventions
=====================

* Column titles are visible and sortable unless a screen marks a column as
  non-sortable.
* In Parties and Party Details, the first click selects a row and updates the
  information panel; activation or the panel action opens the selected party
  or unit. In Deploy Party, selecting an eligible row deploys it immediately.
* Screen controllers provide stable row IDs and resolve them through
  `GameSession`; `TreeItem` objects never hold authoritative game state.
* Search fields, pagination, multi-selection, and inline editing are not part
  of these encampment screens yet.


Deployment eligibility
======================

Only encamped parties with at least one available member appear in the Deploy
Party table. Empty parties, deployed parties, and parties whose members are
all killed or incapacitated do not appear.
