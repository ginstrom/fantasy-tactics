# Bow Ranges Update Plan

## Overview
Update bow weapon ranges in the catalog, ensuring iron and steel shortbows and longbows have their specified maximum ranges, damage values, prices, localizations, and unit details formatting.

## Specifications
- **Iron Shortbow (`shortbow_iron`)**: Range 1–8, Damage 1–6, Price 30
- **Steel Shortbow (`shortbow_steel`)**: Range 1–10, Damage 2–7, Price 90
- **Steel Hunting Bow (`hunting_bow_steel`)**: Range 1–10, Damage 2–7, Price 75
- **Iron Longbow (`longbow_iron`)**: Range 1–12, Damage 1–8, Price 45
- **Steel Longbow (`longbow_steel`)**: Range 1–15, Damage 2–9, Price 135
