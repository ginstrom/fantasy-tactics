# Game Vision

## Tactical D&D style battles

Turn-based tactical combat in a fantasy setting. Think XCOM and D&D have a child.
As a rule set, lean heavily on Basic Fantasy RGP (I've extracted most of the rules as machine-friendly data here: https://github.com/ginstrom/basic-fantasy-rpg-rules-data) for IP reasons, but character development should also be inspired by XCOM/Xenonauts and Fallout 1/2.

## Party management

The player starts with a single party of 4 members, but various research and buildings can increase the number and size of parties. I envision creating specialized parties like scouts, bandit hunting, and dungeon diving.

A rich unit and party UI is required, inspired by XCOM/Xenonauts: Recruitment, development/skill point/equipment, party formation, stats.

## Town management

The player starts out with a bare encampment. As they accumulate gold, they can attract various NPCs such as shopkeepers, blacksmiths, alchemists, and scholars. Various buildings give bonuses, provide gear, unlock spells and other abilities, provide training to recruits, and so on. Items and gold from dungeon diving fund the initial growth of the encampment into a settlement, but eventually the settlement becomes self sufficient and its income outpaces adventuring income. At that point conflicts with neighboring cities become the end game.

The player must tame and settle the area around the settlement for further growth, clearing out bandits and wandering monsters, protecting trade routes, and so on.

Town management needs its own rich UI, although a true town-building experience is not needed. Something like the card-based town building in Rome: Total War is better. For example, building a temple to a certain god will attract clerics of that deity, and leveling up the temple will allow the recruitment of higher-level clerics/paladins and training for low-level recruits.
